Frequently asked questions

The things we get asked most. If your question is not here, email office@rscbuses.ie and we will answer it.

Fully Garda vetted driversOver 10 years in businessAll of Leinster coveredFamily-run business
What areas do you cover?
We are based in Aughrim, Co. Wicklow and cover all of Leinster as standard, including Dublin, Kildare, Carlow, Wexford, Kilkenny and the midlands. We travel further afield too when we have availability on your dates, so it is always worth asking.
How do I book?
We do not take bookings online. Email office@rscbuses.ie or use the enquiry form with your date, group size, pick-up point and destination, and we will confirm availability and price, usually the same day.
What size groups can you take?
Everything from a small private group to a full coach load. We run a full-size touring coach, a midi-coach, Ford Transit minibuses and a Mercedes Sprinter, with 7 drivers, so we can also put more than one vehicle on a larger event. Tell us your numbers and we will match the right vehicle.
Are your drivers vetted?
Yes. Every driver is fully Garda vetted, which matters most for our school work but applies right across the business. All licences and documentation are kept up to date.
Are the buses maintained and insured?
Yes. Our vehicles are serviced and checked regularly, and all required documentation is current. We are happy to provide details to schools, clubs and companies who need them for their own records.
Do you do school transport?
We do. Daily school runs, school tours and one-off outings are a core part of what we do, with vetted drivers and a fleet that is checked regularly.
Can you do early morning airport runs?
Yes. Early departures and late arrivals into Dublin Airport are routine for us. Give us the flight time and we will build the pick-up around it.
Do you run golf trips?
Yes, and it is a growing part of the business. We look after societies, clubs and groups travelling to courses around the country, with space for clubs and bags and a driver for the day.
How far in advance should I book?
As early as you can for weekends, bank holidays and big event dates. Midweek work can often be arranged at shorter notice.
Do you carry stag and hen parties?
We do. Tell us the plan and the numbers and we will quote it.

Need a bus for your group?

Tell us the date, the numbers and where you are going. We will come back to you with a price, usually the same day.
