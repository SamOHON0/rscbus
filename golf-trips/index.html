Golf trips

Travelling as a group

Golf work is a growing part of what we do. One pick-up point, everyone arrives together and the bags travel with the group.

  • Society outings, club away days and corporate golf
  • Courses anywhere in Ireland, single day or overnight
  • Luggage and club space built in
  • Driver available across the day for course, hotel and dinner runs
  • Pick-ups from one point or several along the route
Booking

How it works

1. Tell us the plan

Date, course, group size and where everyone is coming from. A rough plan is enough to start.

2. We price it

We check availability and come back with a price, usually the same day. No deposit taken until you are happy.

3. We do the driving

We confirm timings the week before and collect on the day.

Where we go

Wicklow courses and the rest of the country

Wicklow and the surrounding counties have plenty of courses close to us, and we regularly take groups further afield for a day or a weekend. Tell us the course and we will work out the timings.
